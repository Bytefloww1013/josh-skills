description: "Restate in your own words the user's goal and the problem behind it, so they can correct you before work starts. Use for /in-your-own-words, at the start of a plan's first question round, or when a request is large, vague or could be read more than one way. Not for a small, clear task."
---

Restate in your own words what you think the user's goals are and what problem they're trying to solve.

- **Goal:** what the user wants to be true when the work is done, in one or two sentences.
- **Problem:** what is wrong or missing now that makes them want it.
- **Assumptions:** what you are taking as given that they didn't say. List only the ones that would change the work if they were wrong.

Use your own words, not theirs. Repeating the request back proves nothing. Then ask the user to correct anything you got wrong, and wait for the answer before you plan or build on your reading.
